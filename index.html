Word Formation Challenge

How this works

  • You'll be given a set of prefixes, roots, and suffixes. Combine two or more of them to build a real (or clearly rule-formed) English word.
  • Type only one word at a time into the box.
  • A word is only checked when you press Enter — nothing happens while you're still typing, so keep entering word after word.
  • If it's valid, you score 1 point, and you'll get a quick multiple-choice question about what the word means. Answer correctly for 1 more point.
  • Maximum: 2 points per word. Each word only counts once — retyping a word you already found earns nothing extra.
  • The clock keeps running during the quiz question, so don't dawdle!
  • You have 5 minutes. You'll see how many of the total possible words you've found as you go.
